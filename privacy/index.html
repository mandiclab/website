Privacy

MandićLab is one person, in Serbia, making open-source hardware. This page says what happens to your data here and when you buy something. It is short, because there is not much of it.

This website

The site is a pile of static files. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and tracks nothing. The fonts are served from here, not from Google. Like every website, it sits on a host — GitHub Pages — which sees the requests it serves, and that is the whole of it.

Videos

Previews and tutorials are YouTube players, embedded on the project pages. A player only loads once you open the tab it sits in. From that moment YouTube can see you and may set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy rather than this one. Nothing else on the site reaches outside.

When you buy

Payment and delivery go through Lemon Squeezy, who sell as the merchant of record. You give your payment details to them, not to us, and we never see your card number. What reaches us is your name, your email address, your country and postcode, the last four digits of the card and what you bought. We use it to deliver the files, to answer you if you write, and to keep our own books straight. Lemon Squeezy keeps its own copy under its privacy policy.

When you write to us

Email, WhatsApp and Discord messages stay where you sent them, and we keep them so we can pick up where we left off. You are not added to a mailing list, because there is no mailing list.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, rent it, trade it or hand it to anyone who asks. We do not build a profile of you. Nothing here is designed to follow you anywhere.

How long we keep it

Order records stay as long as bookkeeping requires. Messages stay while they are useful, and go when they are not.

Your rights

Ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for it to be corrected or deleted: write to contact@mandiclab.com and you get an answer from the person who runs this, not from a ticket system. For anything tied to a payment, Lemon Squeezy holds their copy and you can ask them too.
