drivepad

Open source

Drivepad is an open-source handheld sim racing device that you can make at home without special tools, skill or knowledge. It puts the feel of a full sim racing setup right in your hands, in a device no bigger than a gamepad. It works with most racing games.

Support open source

Drivepad is free and open source, and it will stay that way. If you’d like to support it, you can do so on Ko-fi. It helps keep this and future open-source projects going.

Specs

Drivepad consists of:

Steering wheel
900° rotation, horn, no force feedback
Throttle pedal
Clutch pedal
Brake pedal
Paddle shifters
H-pattern shifter
7 gears and reverse
Handbrake
Navigation buttons

Info

A preview, detailed build tutorial, and demo are available on YouTube.

Drivepad was designed so its parts can be printed even on very small 3D printers. If you do not have access to a 3D printer, the printed parts can be ordered from PCBWay.
