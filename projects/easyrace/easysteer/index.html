easysteer

Digital product

easysteer is a fully 3D printable DIY sim racing steering wheel that you can make at home without special tools, skill or knowledge.

The assembly tutorials are still being filmed. They go up on YouTube by 5 November, which is about as long as it takes to print the parts and wait for the electronics, so you can start today and the videos will be there by the time you need them. MandićLab is one person: the same hands do the modelling, the electronics, the firmware, this site, the photos, the filming and the editing. Video is the slowest of those, and we would rather it be good than early.

900 degrees, lock to lock: two and a half turns, like a real car. The horn is in the middle of the hub, where it belongs. The column tilts through four settings: upright for a truck, low and flat for a GT car, and two in between. The clamp that holds it to the desk comes with it.
