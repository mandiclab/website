easyrace

Digital product

Easyrace is a fully 3D printable DIY sim racing setup that you can make at home without special tools, skill or knowledge.

Easyrace consists of:
easysteer — steering wheel
easydrive — pedals
easyshift — H-pattern and sequential shifter
easydrift — handbrake

Unlike any other DIY sim racing equipment, everything here is 3D printed. No screws, nuts, springs, sliders, shafts, bearings or similar mechanical parts are needed.

The electronics are reduced to the minimum, chosen to be affordable and reliable, and easy to source anywhere.

The building process is very simple. Everything has its own place, the amount of parts is reduced to a minimum, no special tools are required and everything is well explained in a video tutorial.
